Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

What the app stores

Your recordings and their titles, dates, lengths and noise settings are saved only on your iPhone, in Side B's own app storage. They are included in your device backups like other app data.

What the app sends

Nothing. Side B has no server and no account, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording a side, after you have allowed access. You can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Deleting your data

Swipe a side in the Crate to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything.
